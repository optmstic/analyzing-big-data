Portugal’s air connectivity · Analyzing Big Data 2026–2027

8

Lisbon flies directly to 106 airports and reaches 562 with one stop: 94% of all the airports in the data. Porto has fewer direct flights, 77, but reaches almost as many, 544. So once you accept one stop, Porto is almost as well connected as Lisbon. And of the places Lisbon cannot reach directly, 40% can be reached by changing planes in Istanbul. Honest limit: the data only covers routes that touch Europe, so 94% means 94% of the airports connected to Europe, not of the whole world.
