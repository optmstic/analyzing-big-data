The result. Lisbon flies directly to 106 airports, but with one stop it reaches 562, which is 94% of the network. Porto has 77 direct flights and reaches 91%. So a regional airport is almost as well connected as the capital once you accept one stop. And one hub stands out: Istanbul is a possible stop for 40% of the places Lisbon cannot reach directly.
