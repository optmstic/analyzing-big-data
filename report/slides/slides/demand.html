Demand. Portugal had 67 million air passengers in 2025, 22% more than in 2019. Lisbon handles half of them and Porto almost a quarter. Funchal grew the most since 2019, by 68%. To count the country’s passengers, national passengers are halved, because a domestic passenger is counted at both airports.
