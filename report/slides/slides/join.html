This is the key idea, and the Big Data part. If Lisbon flies to Istanbul, and Istanbul flies to Tokyo, then Lisbon reaches Tokyo with one stop. To find all such trips, the program matches every route’s destination with every other route’s origin. That is the join, and it turns about 6,200 routes into 923,384 possible trips.
