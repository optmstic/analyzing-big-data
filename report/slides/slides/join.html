This is the central idea. The network is a graph, and a journey with one stop is a join of the routes with themselves: the destination of the first flight is the origin of the second. Lisbon has no direct flight to Tokyo, but Lisbon to Istanbul and Istanbul to Tokyo both exist. That join turns about 6,200 routes into more than 900,000 one-stop journeys, which is why I use Spark.
