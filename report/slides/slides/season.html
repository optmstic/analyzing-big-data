The seasonality ratio is the busiest month’s passengers divided by the quietest month’s. Faro’s 4.4 means July has 4.4 times more passengers than January; Lisbon’s 1.5 means it is busy all year. And most big routes fly 80 to 89% full, but Lisbon to Porto and Lisbon to Faro fly only about 72% full: more than a quarter of their seats are empty.
