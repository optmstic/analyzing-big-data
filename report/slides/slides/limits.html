What Spark made possible, and the limits

What Spark made possible

  • A self-join turned about 6,200 routes into 923,384 journeys
  • 42 files read in two statements, each with its own header
  • One engine for files, SQL, a graph and a stream

Limitations

  • Route data ends in 2024; airport data reaches 2026
  • Only routes that touch Europe: “94%” means 94% of the airports connected to Europe
  • One stop means both routes existed that year: no timetables or prices
  • The scenarios are reference lines, not a forecast
